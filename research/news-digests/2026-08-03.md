# News Digest — 3 August 2026

Finance and business news for Malaysian solo entrepreneurs and freelancers. Compiled for the Fince team as background for content, product, and roadmap decisions. Educational summary only, not tax or legal advice.

---

## 1. Gig Workers Act 2025 (Act 872) now in force — mandatory SOCSO for platform workers

The Gig Workers Act 2025 came into full force on 31 March 2026. Platform operators (e-hailing, delivery, and similar) must now deduct SOCSO contributions for gig workers at a rate of **1.25%**, and the Act introduces service-agreement transparency, payment certainty, anti-discrimination protection, and a new Gig Workers Tribunal for disputes. It covers an estimated 1.64 million workers and — critically for Fince's audience — the definition of "gig worker" extends beyond platform-based work to independent freelancers in creative and professional services.

- Source: Human Resources Online — <https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/gig-workers-act-2025-now-in-force-what-malaysia-s-new-rules-mean-for-organisations-in-the-gig-economy>
- Background: ISEAS Perspective — <https://www.iseas.edu.sg/articles-commentaries/iseas-perspective/2026-48-gig-workers-in-malaysia-is-the-new-law-a-good-deal-by-lee-hwok-aun/>

## 2. E-invoicing exemption threshold raised to RM1M — most solo freelancers now exempt

Effective 1 January 2026, LHDN raised the mandatory e-invoicing threshold from RM500,000 to **RM1,000,000** in annual turnover. Businesses (and sole proprietors) below that threshold are exempt and can opt in voluntarily if they invoice large enterprise clients. Phase 4 (RM1M–RM5M) taxpayers are technically in scope from 1 January 2026, but LHDN extended the penalty-free relaxation period through 31 December 2027.

- Source: ClearTax Malaysia — <https://www.cleartax.com/my/en/e-invoicing-malaysia>
- Deep-dive for SMBs/freelancers: Invoice DIY — <https://invoice-diy.com/lhdn-e-invoice-guide>

## 3. Budget 2026: i-Saraan Plus for gig workers, RM60B in SME funding

Budget 2026 introduced **i-Saraan Plus**, an EPF initiative for gig workers where the government matches contributions up to **RM600 per year**. On the SME side, the government committed RM50 billion under the Budget with a further RM10 billion added, bringing total SME funding to roughly RM60 billion; RM1.5 billion is earmarked specifically for MSME digital adoption, on top of BNM's expanded RM34.9 billion SME fund. The SJPP Financing Guarantee Scheme now guarantees up to 80% of bank financing for SMEs.

- Source: Funding Societies Malaysia — <https://blog.fundingsocieties.com.my/budget-2026-malaysia/>
- Gig-economy angle: iMoney — <https://www.imoney.my/articles/is-the-gig-economy-in-malaysia-dying-or-evolving-budget-2026>

## 4. MyKad now doubles as your Tax Identification Number (TIN)

From 1 March 2026, LHDN uses a Malaysian's **MyKad number as their TIN**, and TINs have been auto-assigned to citizens aged 18 and above. Freelancers who have never filed can now log into MyTax/ezHASiL and file Form B by **15 July 2026** — paper filing is no longer offered. Registration with LHDN is expected within 30 days of starting business activity.

- Source: RinggitPlus — <https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/tax/how-to-file-your-taxes-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-ya-2025.html>
- Filing walkthrough: Denpyo — <https://denpyo.com/en/blog/my-mytax-ezhasil-filing-guide-2026>

## 5. One-stop SME portal launched; RM7B disbursed to 213,000+ businesses this year

The government launched a one-stop portal consolidating financing, training, and assistance programmes for SMEs and small entrepreneurs — a response to long-standing complaints about the fragmentation of MSME support. Agencies under the entrepreneurship ministry have already channelled **RM7 billion** to more than **213,000 entrepreneurs and businesses** in 2026 to date. A related workforce measure: employers receive an **RM2,000 upfront grant** under LiKES (Internship Placement Matching Grant) from 1 March 2026.

- Source: The Star — <https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/05/09/govt-to-launch-one-stop-portal-for-smes-to-streamline-financing-training-programmes>
- MSME funding update: The Star — <https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/01/more-businesses-to-receive-msme-funding>
