## 1. Gig Workers Act 2025 is now live — 1.6M workers now covered

The Gig Workers Act 2025 (Act 872) came into force on **31 March 2026**, giving Malaysia's ~1.64 million gig workers (roughly 10% of total employment) statutory recognition, dispute-resolution channels, and tripartite consultation rights. The companion **Gig Workers (Social Security) Regulations 2026** set a **1.25% contribution rate** and route coverage through SOCSO — a major shift for anyone currently treating themselves as fully self-insured.

## 2. MyKad = TIN, and e-Invoicing crosses into the "everyone over RM1M" phase

From **1 March 2026**, every Malaysian citizen aged 18+ has been auto-assigned a Tax Identification Number equal to their MyKad number — the "do I have a TIN yet?" question is now moot. On the invoicing side, **MyInvois Phase 3 (July 2026)** brings all SST-registered businesses into real-time e-Invoicing, and from **1 January 2026** individual e-invoices are mandatory for any transaction above **RM10,000** (consolidated invoices no longer allowed for those). Turnover threshold sits at **RM1M** — most solo freelancers remain exempt, but larger clients are increasingly asking suppliers to issue e-Invoices anyway.

## 3. Form B deadline for YA 2025 is 15 July 2026 — and SESSS/i-Saraan are still under-used

Freelancers and sole props file **Form B** on MyTax; the e-Filing deadline for YA 2025 is **15 July 2026** (paper 30 June). Guides continue to flag two levers most solo earners still miss: **SESSS** (Self-Employment Social Security Scheme) and **EPF i-Saraan** voluntary contributions — both reduce chargeable income *and* build a retirement/injury safety net that self-employed workers historically go without.

## 4. RM117M in MSME programmes open under the 13th Malaysia Plan

SME Corp has opened applications for **eight MSME programmes** under 13MP (2026–2030) with **RM117 million** allocated for 2026. The stated policy target: lift medium-sized firms from 1.6% to **5% of the enterprise base** by 2030 and push MSME GDP contribution toward **RM1 trillion**. For solo founders specifically, the more immediately usable schemes remain the **SME Digitalisation Grant** (50% matching, capped at **RM5,000** via BSN + MDEC-listed TSPs) and **TalentCorp** internship subsidies (RM2,000 advance or 50% up to RM850/month).

## 5. National Strategy for Financial Literacy 2026–2030 (NS2.0) launched

Malaysia has launched **NS2.0**, the second National Strategy for Financial Literacy covering 2026–2030, explicitly naming gig workers and micro-entrepreneurs among priority segments. Expect more BNM/SC-backed programmes and MADANI-branded outreach on retirement, insurance, and cash-flow literacy over the next 12 months — an opening for aligned content and partnerships.

- Source: **Human Resources Online**, "Malaysia launches National Strategy for Financial Literacy 2026–2030 (NS2.0)" — https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/malaysia-launches-national-strategy-for-financial-literacy-2026-2030-ns2-0
