## 1. Gig Workers Act 2025 in full force from 31 March 2026 — platforms must deduct SOCSO

Under the new Act 872, e-hailing, p-hailing and delivery platform providers are required to deduct SOCSO contributions on behalf of gig workers. The law formally recognises gig workers as a distinct workforce class and puts a floor on social protection, though critics (ISEAS analysis) note carve-outs and enforcement gaps.

## 2. Budget 2026 launches i-Saraan Plus — matching EPF contributions for gig workers & freelancers

A new voluntary EPF scheme aimed at self-employed Malaysians (gig workers, e-hailing/p-hailing riders, freelancers). Government matches contributions up to **RM600/year**, capped at **RM6,000 lifetime**. Paired with expanded mandatory SOCSO coverage. Meaningful for anyone previously outside the formal safety net.

- **Source:** RinggitPlus / KWSP (EPF)

- **URL:** https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/w/article/belanjawan-2026-epf-benefits

## 3. E-invoicing threshold raised to RM1 million — most freelancers exempt, but RM10k rule bites

Effective **1 January 2026**, the e-invoicing exemption threshold rose from RM500k to **RM1 million** annual turnover, sparing most solo operators from LHDN's MyInvois system. However, any single transaction above **RM10,000** must still be issued as a standalone e-invoice — no consolidation allowed. Sole proprietors with multiple businesses must sum revenue across all of them for the threshold test.

- **URL:** https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/lhdnm-e-invoice-general-faqs.pdf

## 4. MyKad now doubles as your Tax Identification Number (TIN) from 1 March 2026

LHDN has auto-assigned TINs to all Malaysian citizens aged 18+, using the MyKad number as the single tax ID. Removes a common onboarding friction for first-time freelancers — no more separate TIN registration before filing Form B. Filing deadline unchanged: **30 June** (15 July on e-Filing).

- **Source:** RinggitPlus / MyTaxMate / CukaiMax
- **URL:** https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/tax/how-to-file-your-taxes-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-ya-2025.html
- **URL:** https://mytaxmate.my/freelancer-tax-guide

## 5. RM60 billion SME package + one-stop portal for solo entrepreneurs

Budget 2026 allocated RM50 billion for SMEs, topped up by another RM10 billion. Notable line items for solo operators: **RM2.5 billion** in micro-loans via TEKUN and BSN, **RM150 million** for digital adoption (MDEC GoDigital MADANI targeting 50,000 SMEs), and **RM650 million** for women/youth entrepreneurs. The Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives Ministry has launched a one-stop portal consolidating financing, training and support programmes across ministries.

- **Source:** The Star / Funding Societies MY / Accounting Firm Penang
- **URL:** https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/05/09/govt-to-launch-one-stop-portal-for-smes-to-streamline-financing-training-programmes
- **URL:** https://blog.fundingsocieties.com.my/budget-2026-malaysia/
