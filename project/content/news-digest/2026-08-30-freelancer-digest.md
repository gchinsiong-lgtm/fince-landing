# Fince News Digest — Malaysian Freelancers & Solo Founders

**Compiled:** 2026-08-30
**Audience:** Malaysian freelancers, sole proprietors, gig workers, solo founders

---

## 1. Gig Workers Act 2025 (Act 872) is now in force

Malaysia's Gig Workers Act 2025 officially came into force on **31 March 2026**, extending legal protection to more than 1.6 million platform and non-platform gig workers — including creative freelancers. Platform providers must now enrol workers in the Self-Employment Social Security Scheme (SKSPS) at a **1.25% contribution rate**, moving PERKESO coverage from opt-in to automatic for platform-based work.

## 2. E-Invoice Phase 3 hits mid-market freelancers on 1 July 2026

The LHDN e-Invoice mandate expands again: businesses (including freelancers) with annual revenue between **RM150,000 and RM1 million** must issue e-invoices in real time from **1 July 2026**. Those under RM150k stay exempt; those above RM1m are already in. Non-compliance carries **RM1,000–RM10,000** per-invoice penalties, and LHDN aggregates income across all sole proprietorships under one MyKad when testing the threshold.

- **Source:** MyTaxMate
- **URL:** https://mytaxmate.my/e-invois-freelancer-malaysia
- **Secondary:** Invoice DIY LHDN Guide — https://invoice-diy.com/lhdn-e-invoice-guide

## 3. MyKad becomes your Tax Identification Number

From **1 March 2026**, LHDN automatically assigns a Tax Identification Number (TIN) to every Malaysian citizen aged 18+, with the **MyKad number now serving as the TIN**. Self-employed individuals earning above RM35,000/year still need to file Form B by 30 June, but registration friction drops for newly-independent workers.

- **Source:** RinggitPlus
- **URL:** https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/tax/how-to-file-your-taxes-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-ya-2025.html

## 4. Budget 2026 launches i-Saraan Plus for gig workers

EPF's new **i-Saraan Plus** scheme matches up to **RM600/year** in contributions (capped at RM6,000 lifetime) for gig, e-hailing and p-hailing workers — layered on top of the existing i-Saraan for all self-employed Malaysians. Budget 2026 also earmarked **RM50 billion** for SME financing, including **RM2.5 billion** for micro-loans through TEKUN and BSN.

- **Source:** KWSP (EPF Malaysia)
- **URL:** https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/w/article/belanjawan-2026-epf-benefits
- **Secondary:** money.com.my freelancer guide — https://money.com.my/guides/freelancer-self-employed-epf-tax-malaysia

## 5. One-stop SME portal + expanded digitalisation grants

The government is launching a **single portal** consolidating SME financing, training and grant programmes, cutting the current maze of agencies. Budget 2026 also renewed **RM1.5+ billion** for MSME digital adoption grants, and TalentCorp is offering SMEs either an **RM2,000 advance** or a **50% intern-wage match (capped at RM850/month)** for up to 10 interns.

- **Source:** The Star
- **URL:** https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/05/09/govt-to-launch-one-stop-portal-for-smes-to-streamline-financing-training-programmes
- **Secondary:** ETD Digital SME Digitalisation Grant — https://etddigital.com/sme-digitalisation-grant-malaysia-2026/
